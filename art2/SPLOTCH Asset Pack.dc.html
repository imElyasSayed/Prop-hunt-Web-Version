02 — Runtime overlays

04 — Build target

Canvas Styleframe — "The Studio"

The intended 3D look for a game room: near-white walls and floor, low-poly flat-shaded props, and colour used sparingly in the environment so painted players pop. Light reads from the top-left (top faces lightest, right faces darkest). This is the mood & silhouette target for the scene build.

The Studio — main Canvas
Props: shelf, locker, sofa, centre pillar, crate, desk, barrel, potted plant. Two Splotchy blobs shown — one camouflaged near the crate, one exposed by the desk.
Alt Canvas — "Neon Alley"
Moodier night map: splat-black shell, neon signage, glow from below — a lit blob stands out hard.
Colour discipline

The environment stays near-white with one accent per prop (cyan locker, violet sofa, tangerine pot). Every other saturated colour on screen is wet paint — player camo, decals, splats. That contrast is the whole read: painted players and fresh splats are the only loud things in a quiet room.

Why it works
Sparse environment colour = maximum pop for painted Chameleons.
